# Commerce Hub

## Register

product

## Users

Ecommerce sellers managing shared warehouse stock and listings across Amazon, eBay, and Shopify. The initial user works at a desk during a normal business day, checking orders and correcting inventory with frequent interruptions.

## Product Purpose

Keep one authoritative stock ledger, link marketplace listings to internal SKUs, reserve and release units reliably, and make sync outcomes visible. This is a local workbench with real account authorization and read-only connection checks. Inventory synchronization and listing edits remain local/simulated until live operations are implemented.

## Brand Personality

Calm, practical, precise. The user confirmed readable tables and a restrained workspace with keyboard and reduced-motion support.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative analytics, invented performance data, marketing-style dashboards, unnecessary modal dialogs, and controls that pretend to connect stores.

## Design Principles

- Stock changes must explain their effect and leave an audit trail.
- Show the difference between physical, reserved, and sellable units.
- Make incomplete connections and simulated results unmistakable.
- Keep common actions beside their relevant records.
- Preserve user input on errors.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic forms and tables, named controls, keyboard focus, text status labels, high contrast, and reduced-motion support. Target WCAG AA; formal certification has not been performed.
